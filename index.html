See it in under a minute

Your first tax invoice, a receipt read for you, your BAS figures and the five countries it's set up for.

Know who owes you, and chase them politely

Every invoice carries your ABN, the GST and the "Tax invoice" heading. Unpaid invoices hang on the dashboard at their due date, stamped with how late they are, above this month's cash book and your GST for the quarter.

  • Email an invoice in one click, with the PDF attached in classic Outlook
  • Chase late payers with a friendly, firm or final reminder
  • Quotes that turn into invoices, and invoices that repeat monthly

Drop in a receipt and it fills in the expense for you

Drag a photo or PDF of a receipt onto the Money page. Wattlebooks reads the supplier, date, amount and GST, and highlights what it filled so you can check it.

Uses the text reader built into Windows, so the receipt never leaves your computer.

Your BAS figures, ready when the quarter ends

Reports work out the boxes you fill in on your BAS for any quarter or financial year, plus a profit and loss. Save them as PDF or CSV for your accountant.

Snap receipts on site with your phone

The optional phone app sends receipt photos and quick invoices back to your computer, and shows who still owes you. It works offline and catches up when you have signal.

To set it up, sign in under Settings > Phone in Wattlebooks on your computer. Then open app.wattlebooks.work on your phone, sign in with the same email and add it to your home screen.

Point your phone's camera here to open the phone app.

Works in the browser on iPhone and Android. Your computer stays the only full copy of your books.

Things people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no trial, no paid plan and no ads. It's open source under the MIT licence, and you can read every line on GitHub.

How is it different from Xero, MYOB or QuickBooks?

Those are monthly subscriptions that run online, with extras like bank feeds and payroll. Wattlebooks is free, keeps your books on your own computer and sticks to invoices, quotes, expenses and your GST. If you need payroll or bank feeds, one of those is the better fit.

Where are my invoices kept?

In a folder on your own computer. Nothing goes online unless you sign in to the phone app, and then only your open invoices, recent payments and client names are shared with your phone.

Can I use it outside Australia?

Yes. It has built-in tax rules for New Zealand, the UK, Canada and the US as well, and you can bill clients in any country in 13 currencies. Quebec QST and the provincial sales taxes in BC, Saskatchewan and Manitoba aren't covered yet.

Do I need the phone app?

No. It's an optional extra and the desktop app does everything on its own. If you want it, sign in under Settings > Phone on your computer, then open app.wattlebooks.work on your phone and sign in with the same email. It's free while it's a pilot.

Does it work on a Mac?

There's a Mac version marked as a preview, for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and a Linux version too. Receipt scanning is Windows only for now.

Why does Windows warn me when I open it?

It won't if you install from the Microsoft Store, since Microsoft signs that copy. The plain .exe isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say it protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway. The code is on GitHub if you want to check it first.

I use the .exe. Can I switch to the Store version?

Yes, and your books come with you. Close the .exe, then install Wattlebooks from the Microsoft Store. If you'd moved your data to OneDrive, Dropbox or Google Drive, the Store version finds it by itself. If it opens with empty books instead, go to Settings > Your data, choose Use data from another PC and pick the InvoiceDesk folder. Type %LOCALAPPDATA% into the folder box to jump there. Nothing is copied or deleted, so just don't run both versions at once.

Does it replace my accountant?

No. It keeps your records and works out your GST figures, but it isn't tax advice. Check your BAS with your accountant or the ATO if you're unsure.

Wasn't this called InvoiceDesk?

Yes. InvoiceDesk is now Wattlebooks, with a new look. Your invoices, clients and settings carry over as they are, and the Windows download is now Wattlebooks.exe.
